Mochi Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

The short version: Mochi has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your food diary stays on your iPhone and, if you leave iCloud backup on, in your own private iCloud. The developer never receives it and has no access to it.

What Mochi stores

Your diary (foods, portions and meals), saved foods and recipes, budget and macro goals, weights you enter, and app settings. All of it is stored on your iPhone.

iCloud backup

When iCloud backup is on (the default), Mochi saves a copy of your diary in the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account. It's protected by your iCloud account's security (and end-to-end encrypted if you've turned on Advanced Data Protection). The developer has no access to it. Readings that come from Apple Health are left out of this backup. You can turn iCloud backup off in Mochi → Settings → Backup. To delete a backup, go to the Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Mochi.

You can also export a backup file. That file goes wherever you choose to send or save it.

Household sharing

Household sharing is optional and off until you start or join a household. When it's on, Mochi uses Apple's iCloud sharing to share your recipes, the foods you entered yourself, your label fixes and the first name you choose with the people you invite. If you send someone a meal, the foods and amounts in that meal go to them too. Your diary, weight, goals and Apple Health data are never shared. Shared items live in the iCloud account of the person who started the household, and only the people in it can see them; the developer has no access. Meals you send are removed from iCloud after a week. You can leave or stop sharing at any time in Mochi → Settings → Household; everyone keeps a copy of what was shared before.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you connect it, Mochi writes the energy, protein, carbohydrates and fat you log, and reads your weight and, if you turn on workout calories, your workouts' active energy. When you set a weight goal and choose to fill it in from Health, Mochi also reads your age, sex, height and recent step counts to fill in your profile and suggest an activity level. Health data is used only to show and adjust your diary and budget inside the app. It is never used for advertising, never sold or shared, and never stored in iCloud by Mochi. You can change access at any time in the Health app → your profile → Apps → Mochi.

Services Mochi contacts

  • Open Food Facts. When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a public, non-profit food database, to look up nutrition. No account or identifier is sent with it. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • Recipe links. When you import a recipe, Mochi downloads the web page you entered, much like a browser would, to read its ingredients. Nothing from your diary is sent.
  • USDA data is built into the app and needs no connection.
  • Apple Intelligence. When a recipe page has no structured recipe data, or you paste a recipe as text, Mochi asks Apple Intelligence to pick out the recipe and name its ingredients. That text is processed on your iPhone or, where available, by Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which uses it only to answer the request and doesn't store it. Your diary is never sent.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes and, when you choose to, to photograph a nutrition label. Label photos (and any you pick from your library) are read on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence and Apple's text recognition; they are not saved or sent anywhere.

Widgets

The home-screen widget reads a small summary of today (calories eaten, your budget and Mochi's look) that the app saves on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Deleting Mochi removes everything stored on your iPhone. To remove the iCloud backup too, delete it from iCloud storage as described above. Data Mochi wrote to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Children

Mochi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

Calorie budgets and nutrition values are estimates for general information. They aren't medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional about your diet, especially if you have a medical condition or a history of disordered eating.
