Moving to a new iPhone

With iCloud backup on (Settings → Backup), install Mochi on the new iPhone, signed in to the same iCloud account, and it offers to restore your diary. You can also use Settings → Backup → Export backup file, then Restore from file on the new iPhone.

Apple Health

Connect in Settings → Apple Health. Mochi saves the food you log to Health and reads your weight and, if you turn on workout calories, your workouts. Change access in the Health app → your profile → Apps → Mochi.

Reading recipes and labels

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Mochi can read a recipe you paste or a photo of a nutrition label, on your iPhone. Always check the result against the original before saving.

Mochi's body changes

Mochi gets rounder, shinier or buffer with what you eat. If you'd rather he kept his shape, turn off Settings → Mochi's body changes.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything on your iPhone. To remove the iCloud backup, open the Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Mochi.
